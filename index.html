About the Shed

Powering
Western
India

Diesel Loco Shed, Sabarmati is a premier locomotive maintenance facility under the Ahmedabad Division of Indian Railways' Western Railway zone. Located 6 km east of Ahmedabad Junction, adjacent to the historic Sabarmati Ashram, the shed serves as the maintenance hub for both freight and passenger train fleets.

One of three diesel loco sheds in the Western Railway zone, Sabarmati holds the distinction of being one of the last remaining major metre-gauge locomotive sheds in India, while simultaneously operating a large broad-gauge EMD fleet with 125+ WDG-4 locomotives.

All locomotives are received from Banaras Locomotive Works (BLW) and undergo scheduled maintenance, heavy repairs, and periodic overhauls at the shed's fully-equipped workshop.

Milestones

A History
of Service

1978
Shed Commissioned
Diesel Loco Shed, Sabarmati was established on 8 June 1978 under Western Railway's Ahmedabad Division with an initial holding of 55 YDM-3 and YDM-5 metre-gauge locomotives.
1998
Full Capacity Reached
The shed reached a full loco holding capacity and became one of the most significant MG loco sheds in Western India, supporting major metre-gauge operations across Gujarat.
2008
Broad Gauge Transition
50 WDG-4 broad-gauge locomotives were added as part of the shed's transition from metre-gauge to a mixed-gauge facility, heralding a new era of high-horsepower traction.
2009
First EMD Loco Arrives
The first EMD locomotive — WDG-4 No. 12224 — arrived at Sabarmati shed in July 2009, marking the beginning of large-scale EMD fleet operations. The dedicated EMD workshop was made fully operational.
2012
WDG-4D Prototype Assignment
The first WDG-4D prototype (No. WDG-4-668) was assigned to Sabarmati shed, making it one of the first sheds to operate this next-generation dual-cab freight locomotive.
2024
International Export — Togo
Nine YDM-4 metre-gauge locomotives from Sabarmati shed were sold to Togorail SA and shipped to Togo, representing a landmark international milestone for Indian Railways locomotive exports.
